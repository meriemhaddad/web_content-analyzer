💡 Common Issues & Solutions

403 Forbidden: Website blocks automated access. Try accessing the URL manually first, or the site may require login/cookies.

404 Not Found: URL doesn't exist. Check for typos or if the page has been moved.

429 Rate Limited: Too many requests. Our system automatically retries with delays.

503 Service Unavailable: Website is temporarily down. Try again later.
